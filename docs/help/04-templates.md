- A **Template** is a ready-made slide set: layout, layer styles, brand colors and general settings such as default size and locale.
- The **Templates** gallery ships with complete sets for popular app types (health & fitness, finance, productivity, education, shopping & food) plus the **Welcome tour**. They come with demo screenshots so you can judge the design: replace them with your own by dropping an image on each phone.
- **Use template** creates a **new project** from it. **Add to this project** appends its slide groups to the project you have open, which is handy for pulling in one layout without starting over. Brand colors a template brings along are added to your Brand Kit, so its color references keep working.
- Turn your own project into a template with **Export**. Exported templates never include your screenshots or images: image layers come through as empty placeholders for whoever imports the file.
- **Import** loads a `.template.json` file and appends it to the current project.
- The first time you open PixelDeck, the Welcome tour is created for you as a normal project. You can delete it, and bring it back at any time from the gallery.
